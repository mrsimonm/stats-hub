*two types of relationships:
*deterministic (functional) dependence – in
sciences and mathematics
*one value of the first variable unambiguously
corresponds to one value of the other variable (no
randomness, no uncertainty)
*statistical (probabilistic) – in business and
economics
*one value of the first variable may correspond
to several values of the other variable
*described by regression and correlation analysis
=== SLIDE 3 ===
*regression analysis:
*describes unilateral dependencies
*cause: independent (explanatory) variable X
*consequence: dependent variable Y
*describes the course (shape) of (any) relationship
by a mathematical function
*correlation analysis:
*describes mutual dependencies
*variables X and Y are “equal”
*focuses on the strength (and direction) of a linear
relationship only
