*probability is a specific way of thinking
reflecting the fact that some things may
happen (occur) with higher or lower
“maybe” (i.e. uncertainty)
*even probability (as a phenomenon of
human thinking) has its own “building

=== SLIDE 3 ===
*experiment– process that (having been
carried out) results in one of two or more
possible outcomes
*e.g. tossing a fair 6-sided die (six possible
outcomes)
*outcome– a particular result of an
experiment
*e.g. number 6 falls
*random experiment (random trial) – its
outcome cannot be predicted with
certainty; under the same conditions, it
results in different outcome(s)
3
=== SLIDE 4 ===
*event– collection of one or more of the
outcomes of an experiment
*capital letters: A, B, …
*A– number 6 falls in one toss: favourable
outcome is only 6
*A– number lower than 4 falls in one toss:
favourable outcomes are 1, 2, 3
*probability– numerical measure of the
likelihood that a specific event will occur
*P(A), P(B), …
4
=== SLIDE 5 ===
*certain event: under given conditions is
certain to occur with the probability equal to
unity
*E, P(E) = 1 (i.e. 100 %)
*impossible event: under given conditions
cannot occur; has a zero probability
*Ø, P(Ø) = 0
*random event: will occur with a certain
probability between zero and unity (it may
occur or not); it is a result of a random
experiment
*A, B, …; 0 < P(A) < 1
