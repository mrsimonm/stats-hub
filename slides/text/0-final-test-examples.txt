11. The mean growth coefficient of a time series may never be lower than 1.
12. After increasing all the values in a data set by the same constant (number), the value of median
(calculated from these values) will change.

of the dependent variable empirical values from the values on a regression function.
14. The value of the test statistic G (ie. g), which is used when assessing independence in a contingency
table, is always non-negative (i.e. either zero or positive).
15. The probability of the union of two disjoint random events is only sometimes (i.e. not always)
equal to the sum of individual probabilities of those events.
16. A high variation of values in a data set increases the relevance of their arithmetic mean (i.e. we may
have better idea about the magnitudes of all the values in the analysed data set knowing the value of

analysed data set.
21. The inflation rate is a change in the price index expressed absolutely (i.e. by the absolute difference).
22. The probability of type I error is always higher than the probability of type II error.
23. The criterion for the decision whether to use composite or aggregate indices

24. In a chi-square goodness-of-fit test, we assess (test) the equality (conjunction) of the observed

25. If the calculated value of a test statistic (in a statistical hypothesis test) falls into the rejection
