=== SLIDE 25 ===
*Measures of absolute variation:
*in the same (or similar) units as the statistical variable that is
analyzed
*do not allow comparison of the magnitude of variation for
variables with a different level (assuming only positive values:
higher level => higher measures of absolute variation)
*Measures of relative variation:
*measure variation in relation to the level of the variable that is
analyzed
*dimensionless numbers
*assuming only positive values: allow comparison of the magnitude
of variation for any variables (even with different units)
