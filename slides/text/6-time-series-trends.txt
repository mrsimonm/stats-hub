=== SLIDE 4 ===
*widely used in finance, economics,…
*we calculate the trend component separately in
short segments (“windows”) of a time series by
some type of the mean (average)
*we move forward with the window (starting at the
beginning of a time series) through the time series
by excluding the first value (in the window) and
adding the next one
*window’s length (length of a moving segment) m:
*odd number: m = 2p + 1, p = 1, 2, 3, …
*even number: m = 2p
4
=== SLIDE 5 ===
5
*selection of m
*subjective in the case of a time series without a
seasonal component
*the stronger smoothing we want, the higher m
we should select
*in the case of a seasonal component in a time
series, we should select m equal to the length
of seasonality s (or 2s, 3s etc.)
*quarterly time series (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4; s = 4): m = 4
*monthly time series (M1, M2, …, M12; s = 12): m = 12
*daily time series (Mo, Tu, …, Su; s = 7): m = 7
