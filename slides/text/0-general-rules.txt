4ST601 Course Rules
Course participants are advised to conform to the following rules
1. The lecture/seminar attendance capacity cannot be extended beyond the capacity visible
during registrations.
2. The students’ assessment is carried out on the basis of two mid-term written tests and
one final written test taken in the examination period. Candidates are required to score
at least 16 points (i.e. 40 per cent) in mid-term tests (maximum 40 points). If they
fail, they are not allowed to sit for the final test and receive the mark 4.
3. Candidates are allowed to use only officially available formulas and tables for both
mid-term and final tests. (If the formulas or tables are modified in any sense or
replenished with text, they may be taken away from the student without compensation
by the supervisor.) All electronic devices (mobile phones, watches, earpods, …) are
strictly forbidden during the tests; you will have to place them in a designated area for
the duration of the test. Any violation of the given rules during the tests (or any other
form of cheating, or attempted cheating) will directly lead (at least) to final grade 4
(fail).
4. Excuse from the whole course for non-attendance during the semester. The doctor’s
certificate (sick note) valid for at least four weeks during a particular semester is
required. The course may be excused only by the lecturer. The sick note has to be sent
by email to the lecturer (and in copy to student’s coordinator) and the lecturer has to be
notified about its termination immediately. An excuse has to be submitted and approved
within the first week of the examination period at the latest.
5. For the students of the Faculty of Economics: Those who have already failed to
complete the 4ST601 course during their current studies (i.e. the students who have at
least once received a failing grade 4) may be excused from the course on their own
written request (by email to the lecturer and seminar leader in a copy) during the
semester before the regular term of the 2nd mid-term test at the latest.
6. If a student reports sickness during the semester, but succeeds in passing the mid-term
tests in alternate terms, he/she is allowed to take the final test. Alternate terms of the
mid-term tests are administered within the first week of the examination period at the
latest.
7. Excuse from the whole course for absence during the examination period. If a
student wants to apologize for the absence due to illness during the examination period,
the same provisions as for an excuse during the semester are applicable (see par. 4). The
candidate may be excused unless he/she passes any exams (in other subjects) while
being off sick, the doctor’s certificate having been sent by email to the lecturer (and in
copy to student’s coordinator). The course may be excused only by the lecturer.
8. Excuse from the final test. An excuse may be accepted only on the basis of a sickness
note submitted to the lecturer within three working days from the final to-be-excused
test’s date at the latest. Excuse for not attending the last term of the final test does not
establish the claim for the excuse of the whole course.
